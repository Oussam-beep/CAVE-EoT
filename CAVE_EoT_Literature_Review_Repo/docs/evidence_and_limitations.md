# Evidence, Limitations and Claim Discipline

## Evidence levels used in this repository

- **Source-derived:** directly stated in the current handbook or its identified paper notes.
- **Proposed:** a design choice, research hypothesis or future experiment for CAVE-EoT.
- **Unresolved:** an ambiguity or fact that the current sources do not establish.
- **Future result:** a measurement that may only be reported after running a documented experiment.

## Do not overclaim

- Do not call excess overlap a probability of copying without separate validation.
- Do not claim to recover hidden causal provenance from text alone.
- Do not equate independent generation with independent knowledge or errors.
- Do not imply that every common step is harmful redundancy.
- Do not claim global novelty from a literature map with unresolved paper identities.
- Do not treat a paper's baseline or ablation as the paper's full main method.
- Do not claim performance improvement, lower cost or validated accuracy before experiments support it.

## Known review limitations

The handbook records 48 paper names, 37 source-identifiable entries and 11 unresolved intended identities. It also notes that some paper-property cells remain unverified and that the last map was a rapid pass. These gaps must be resolved before a final novelty claim.

## Public release caution

Before making the repository public, confirm that the supervisor permits public release of the present research framing and handbook. Do not publish third-party full-text papers, proprietary data or private communications without permission.
